## URL Patterns
Bounce has structured URLs for easy navigation:
- City Luggage storage pages: https://bounce.com/luggage-storage/{city_slug} - Specific luggage storage solutions for each city
- Store listings: https://bounce.com/stores/{city_slug}/{store_slug} - Bounce Partner store locations in each city

Examples:
- https://bounce.com/luggage-storage/london
- https://bounce.com/luggage-storage/london/london-eye
- https://bounce.com/stores/lisbon/wkld0u
- https://bounce.com/luggage-delivery/japan
